# frog-knight-vs-boss-fly

Created by **Rylan Saw** — a retro 16-bit arcade action game playable across **Mobile**, **PC**, and **Web** browsers. Battle through **Stage 1** against swarms of small swamp flies for **50 seconds** until the **Giant Boss Fly** arrives in **Stage 2**, unleash **Giant Slashes**, slash floating items to unlock your **Item Skill** button, and trigger combos & Mega Strikes.

## Author
- **Creator:** Rylan Saw

## Features
- **Stage Progression (50s Boss Timer):** Fight small flies first for 50 seconds before the Giant Boss Fly enters the swamp.
- **Giant Slash & Combo System:** Wide sword slash arcs, combo counters, and Mega Strikes.
- **Slash-to-Unlock Item Skill:** Slash floating items with your sword to unlock and activate special item abilities (`+HP`, `SHD`, `WAV`, `ZAP`, `2X`).
- **Mobile, PC & Web Support:** Responsive arcade cabinet layout with virtual analog joystick, touch buttons, and keyboard controls.
